A browser game

Kartana.

Turn any web page into a shooting game.

Click the Kartana icon on a page, and a stick figure drops in so you can shoot the page to pieces, fight waves of enemies, and collect weapons.

Everything is visual and temporary. Press R or refresh the page, and everything returns to normal.

Controls

A / D — Move
W — Jump
S — Drop through platforms
Mouse — Aim and shoot
1–3 — Switch weapon
R — Restore the page
M — Mute sound
` — Toggle Kartana
