Kartana Privacy Policy Kartana does not collect, sell, share or transmit any personal data or browsing data to the developer or to any third party. It makes no network requests and contains no analytics, advertising or third-party code. What Kartana handles on your device Kartana runs only on the tab where you click its toolbar icon. While the game is running it handles two kinds of information, locally in your browser and only to make the game work: - Website content: it reads the structure of the page (for example the position, size and style of elements, and whether they contain text) so the game knows which parts can be hit and where the stick figure can stand. It changes how the page looks while you play. Pressing R or refreshing the page restores it. - User activity: it reads your keyboard presses and mouse movement and clicks on that page to control the game (moving, aiming and shooting).
