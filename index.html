# Kartana Privacy Policy **Last updated: October 12, 2026** ## Data Collection Kartana does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data or browsing data. ## How Kartana Works Kartana only runs on the browser tab where you click its toolbar icon. While it runs, it reads the layout of that page locally in your browser so the game knows which elements can be hit. This information never leaves your browser, is not saved, and is discarded when you close or refresh the tab. ## Network Requests and Third Parties Kartana makes no network requests and contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party code. ## Contact For questions about this privacy policy, contact: enumah44@gmail.com
